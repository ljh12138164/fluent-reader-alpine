Fluent Reader Alpine

A lightweight edition of Fluent Reader.

A lightweight, independently maintained fork of Fluent Reader by Haoyuan Liu and its contributors. Alpine keeps the familiar reading interface and feed logic, with a Tauri runtime and virtualized article views. Screenshots illustrate the inherited upstream interface.

Open & Organized.

Stay in sync with Inoreader, Feedbin, Miniflux, Nextcloud, or services compatible with Fever or Google Reader API. Alternatively, import your sources from an OPML file and read locally. Organize sources with groups and move between computers with full data backups.

Read fluently.

Read RSS content with the built-in article view. For feeds that only provide snippets, extract full content with Mercury Parser, load the webpage in the app, or open it externally. Choose card, list, magazine or compact layouts with virtualized article views.

Search. Filter.

Search article titles and contents with regular expressions. Mark articles as starred, hidden or unread, and automate these actions with rules as articles arrive.

A lighter foundation.

Tauri uses your system WebView instead of bundling a complete Chromium and Node.js runtime. Rust manages native operations and the local SQLite database. Virtualized views mount articles near the visible area, while pnpm, Rspack and Oxc handle frontend builds and checks. PAC proxy settings apply to native HTTP requests.

Actual resource use depends on your system WebView and feeds. Alpine retains the upstream BSD 3-Clause license and attribution.

Light, dark, or follow your system.

Theme support for both the interface and article reading.
