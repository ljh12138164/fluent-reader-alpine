# Fluent Reader Alpine icon

`icon-alpine.png` is the master raster asset for this fork. It was generated
with the built-in imagegen tool using the upstream `icon.png` as a reference.
The original asset remains unchanged. Upstream attribution belongs to
Fluent Reader by Haoyuan Liu and its contributors; see the root `LICENSE`.

The icon retains the upstream three blue sheets and white F, with a small
rounded green `Lite` badge at the lower right to identify the lightweight
edition. There are no mountain or other replacement design elements.

Selected edit prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the provided original Fluent Reader icon. This is a minimal badge overlay, NOT a redesign. Preserve the exact original three blue overlapping sheets, their positions, sizes, tiny corner radii, original soft shadows, original flat colors and gradients, the white F and its exact typography. Do not redraw, rearrange, stretch, modernize, enlarge or reinterpret the original icon. Do not add mountains. Add ONLY a small green rounded rectangular badge with the exact white text "Lite" at the lower-right corner, over the blank blue area on the right, clear of the F. Badge should be around 27% of the icon width, with bold simple sans-serif lettering and a thin white outline for legibility. Keep the original icon as the dominant shape and preserve its near-square original canvas composition. Fully transparent background outside the icon and badge. One icon only. The result must immediately look like the very same original Fluent Reader icon with a lightweight-edition label added.

Desktop PNG, ICO and ICNS variants in `src-tauri/icons/` are generated with
`pnpm tauri icon`. The documentation uses `docs/imgs/icon-alpine.png`, and
the About panel uses the smaller `dist/icons/alpine.png`.
